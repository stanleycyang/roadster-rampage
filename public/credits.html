Independent by design

Roadster Rampage is an independent, unofficial arcade game inspired by futuristic flying sports cars. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or authorized by Tesla, Inc.

People & AI assistance

  • Project creator: Stanley (@stanleycyang).
  • Release preparation and documentation also assisted by Codex.

Built with open-source tools

  • Three.js — rendering and postprocessing; MIT license.
  • Vite — development and builds; MIT license.
  • TypeScript — type checking; Apache License 2.0.
  • Orbitron and Rajdhani — interface fonts served by Google Fonts; SIL Open Font License 1.1.

The music and sound effects are synthesized with the Web Audio API. Vehicle meshes, scenery, and gameplay textures are generated by the repository's code.
